Sao Béo - Creative Souvenir

"Shine silly - shine bright"

Sao Beo is a creative brand specializing in the design and sale of souvenir products such as stickers, keychains distinguished by its exclusive, hand-drawn, and emotionally expressive illustration style. Featuring the mascot "Chubby Silly Star" - a round, chubby, goofy, and adorable star - Sao Beo delivers small yet meaningful products that carry positive messages, empathy, and emotional encouragement for everyday life. Each design is not only decorative but also a way for young people to express their feelings, personality, and find gentle comfort in a fast-paced world. By celebrating imperfection, authenticity, and simple joy. Sao Beo aspires to become an inspirational brand that naturally connects art, emotions, and people in a warm and approachable way.

Vision

"To become the most beloved creative brand, where imperfection is celebrated."

Sao Beo aims to become a leading creative lifestyle brand in Vietnam, loved by young people and regarded as a familiar part of their everyday emotional life. The brand aspires to serve as a positive emotional solution for Gen Z, helping them find joy, comfort, and a sense of being true to themselves through small yet emotionally rich artistic products. For Sao Beo, imperfection is not a flaw, but what makes people more relatable and lovable. Therefore, its vision is to build a business that is not only commercially successful, but also creates lasting emotional value and positively influences how young people perceive themselves and life.

Mission

"To create small products with the power to soothe the soul."

Sao Beo's mission is to design meaningful products that combine humor and inspiration to brighten the everyday moments of young people. Through hand-drawn artwork and relatable quotes, Sao Beo hopes to send gentle reminders to Gen Z that they are not alone, that their emotions are valid, and that they deserve to be acknowledged and cherished. Each Sao Beo product is created not only for use or decoration, but also to accompany users through happy days, sad days, tiring days, or moments when they simply need a little extra motivation to keep going.

Core Values

"Authentic creativity – Positive impact – Sustainable connection."

Sao Beo builds its operations around five core values: creativity, authenticity, positivity, sustainability, and community connection. Creativity is expressed through original, hand-drawn designs that are never copied, allowing the brand to maintain a distinct identity. Authenticity is reflected in emotions and messages that truly represent what young people experience in their lives. Sao Beo consistently aims to spread positivity, not by imposing perfection, but by encouraging self-acceptance. At the same time, the brand emphasizes sustainable development through the use of environmentally friendly materials and the cultivation of a community where art becomes a bridge - connecting people, encouraging sharing, and inspiring one another.
